Connor Savage

Developer / Canada

Bio

Connor Savage is a Canadian software and hardware developer who started programming at age 10, creating plugins for Grand Theft Auto V's LSPDFR modification. His early projects included FBI Callouts, EMS Callouts, K9 Callouts, and City Callouts.

Since then, his work has expanded into hardware, desktop applications, web platforms, and game development. Projects include DeskOS, Receet, Canada RP, and Dispatch Simulator, ranging from physical touchscreen controllers to digital receipt systems and simulation games.

Projects

DeskOS

deskos.ca

Touchscreen desk controller combining custom hardware with companion software for audio mixing, Spotify integration, system monitoring, and customizable controls.

Receet

receet.ca

NFC-based digital receipt system with point-of-sale integrations, a merchant dashboard, and a mobile app for managing receipts.

Canada RP

canadarp.ca

Canadian FiveM roleplay community founded and operated by Connor. Features OPP, RCMP, Toronto Police, Fire/EMS, and civilian departments, with custom vehicles, scripts, and server systems.

Dispatch Simulator

dispatchsim.ca

911 emergency dispatch simulation game featuring AI-powered callers, unscripted voice conversations, and real-time police, fire, and EMS unit management.

LSPDFR Plugins

LCPDFR Profile

Creator of FBI Callouts, EMS Callouts, K9 Callouts, and City Callouts for LSPDFR, a Grand Theft Auto V modification that allows players to take on the role of a police officer. These plugins introduce additional emergency calls, incidents, and scenarios.
